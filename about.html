Education

Education and Academic Journey

Academic background, doctoral progression, technical preparation, and key milestones.

Rupinder Kaur is a doctoral scholar in Computer Science Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Ropar. Her recent academic profile reflects a steady movement from core computing education into machine learning, computer vision, and applied multimodal analysis.

Her projects and publications show a clear applied direction: thermal video based adulteration detection, RGB and thermal image analysis, plant growth modeling with multiview images, and machine learning workflows for real-world datasets.

Before starting doctoral work, she completed a master's degree in Information Technology at G.G.D.S.D. College, Chandigarh, and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science at D.A.V. College, Jalandhar.
