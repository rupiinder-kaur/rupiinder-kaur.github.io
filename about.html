01 - Profile

Academic background and core strengths

A concise portrait built directly from the updated CV.

Rupinder Kaur is a doctoral scholar in Computer Science Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Ropar. Her recent academic profile reflects a steady movement from foundational computing education into machine learning, computer vision, and applied multimodal analysis.

The project and publication record in the CV shows interest in both theory and application: from least squares, PCA, SVD, and kernel methods to GDP analysis, thermal image based adulteration detection, and plant growth modeling with multiview images.

Before starting doctoral work, she completed a master's degree in Information Technology at G.G.D.S.D. College, Chandigarh, and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science at D.A.V. College, Jalandhar.
